# Campaign design V8: 100 planned, 5 implemented

## Rules the player can trust
Ivory ceramic tiles accept the whole portal aperture; charcoal metal never does. This is a visual/material rule, not arbitrary hidden labels. Wall, floor and moving surfaces use the same placement fit check. Seams are shallow construction joints, not collision gaps. An attached portal follows its plate. Rigid-object identity persists. Gravity adds speed while falling; portals rotate the velocity vector instead of replacing it with a scripted launch. A held friend travels in the same transform. Only the grounded player and the real nearby friend complete the goal. No checkpoints, no timed ad interruptions during physical motion.

## The first five
1. **Два берега**: two wall tiles, safe trench, destination friend. Learn movement, aiming and a connected pair; no pad/lift/ornamental props. Falling into the trench has a recovery staircase, not a mandatory restart.
2. **Обратный путь**: one pressure platform, one door, an L-shaped baffle. The floor portal is prepared under the friend, the player passes the live door and opens the receiving wall portal. Retrieving the friend removes the weight and closes the circuit. Not a permanently latched delivery button.
3. **Падение — это разгон**: a tall balcony, a floor tile and a side-wall tile facing a separated low island. Walking into the portal has insufficient energy; a gravity fall carries both player and friend across. Recovery steps return to the balcony. No hidden launch pad.
4. **Подвижный адрес**: a visible terminal, real lift deck and mounted portal tile, upper gallery. Put a portal on the low plate before commanding the lift. Its exit moves upstairs; stable endpoints allow thinking rather than reflex timing. No duplicate visible collision deck.
5. **Угол решает**: a gravity well and an articulated tilted exit, separate raised island. Combine height with direction: an upright exit is too low, a 35-degree incline reaches the landing. The terminal physically rotates the existing model and attached portal.

## Purposeful inventory
Player, friend and device are core. Wall tiles establish the rule. Floor tiles introduce vertical transfer. Pressure platform carries weight and is itself a portal surface. Lift changes the address of an attached exit. Terminal controls the adjacent mechanism. Rotating panel controls momentum direction. No chairs, pianos, energy cells, fans or unrelated showcases in these five rooms. They remain in the source library, not in the active rooms or downloaded package.

## Proposed later blocks — not implemented
06–10: repeat spatial transfer with fewer obvious sightlines, optional alternate solutions and no new machinery.
11–20: rolling objects, ramps, braking/capture basins; introduce a shaped catch cradle, not another copy of the cube.
21–30: a weight-balanced seesaw and centre of mass; portals transfer the load, not a switch state.
31–40: anchored motion, speed-relative traversal and conservation audits for moving exits.
41–50: fan airflow redirected through portals; show direction with light particles and respond to object mass.
51–60: shutters and reversible mechanical sequencing, no hidden timers.
61–70: moving payloads and catches, retaining angular velocity.
71–80: one reusable pair across larger interconnected rooms, explicit return routes.
81–90: combinations and alternative physical solutions, not arbitrary stricter precision.
91–100: mastery rooms with all rules already learned.

New rules are introduced free. Optional hint tiers move from observation to causal explanation to explicit solution. Do not intentionally break clarity or require advertisements to learn controls or to finish a level.
